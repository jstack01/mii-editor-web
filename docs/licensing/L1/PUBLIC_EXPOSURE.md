## Available remedies — owner must choose explicitly

1. **Obtain specific written permission:** identify old-version hashes, author contributions, inherited/Nintendo content, font provenance, source/history, binary packages/containers, modification and fork/public-hosting/commercial redistribution scope. A noncommercial or no-redistribution reply would not meet the desired broadly reusable MIT/full-app goal. Preserve proof and accurately scope it; no response is not consent.
2. **Use established licensed components:** document component matches to the author's later MIT sources, carry original notices, and rebase only those components after approval. This is not a shortcut to all the old artwork or trademarks. Resolve any fonts/separate contributors independently.
3. **Replace unresolved assets and source expression:** independently owned/licensed art, GUI labels/font and, where needed, independent implementations of functional behavior; regenerate screenshots/packages and reverify visual/functional differences. The original goal of faithful old artwork may conflict with freely redistributable complete-app clearance. No replacement/redesign is performed in this audit.
4. **Separate a truly redistributable code-only offering:** eligible code under MIT, excluding unresolved catalogs/images/ports until cleared. Clearly say it is not a complete functioning graphics edition and do not bundle a downloader that disguises unresolved distribution. Don't claim that the existing complete preview is all MIT.
5. **Reduce public exposure if rights cannot be verified:** possible options include removing affected future artifacts, retiring selected releases/assets or GHCR versions, changing availability, or a reviewed history-rewrite/separate-clean-repository process. These are consequential and need explicit approval. Nothing is deleted or rewritten here. Removing HEAD files alone does not remove older commits/releases/layers; history cleanup cannot recall forks, downloads, cached archives or third-party mirrors. Ask GitHub about caches only if a separately approved remediation requires it; do not promise complete erasure.
6. **Professional scope/risk review:** a qualified adviser may identify a lawful basis or practical boundary without every requested permission, depending on jurisdictions and actual content. This audit does not assert a fair-use exemption, a trademark infringement finding or a universal requirement for a particular license. It does identify that a claim of established full-app redistribution rights is presently unsupported.

The owner originally accepted historical accessibility; this audit explains its additional exposure without reversing that choice. Existing preview releases are not silently declared withdrawn or newly licensed. Pages remains optional and blocked on a **separate** future approval after review; it is not a prerequisite for 1.0.

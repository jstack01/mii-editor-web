## AI and human contribution limits

OpenAI's [Terms of Use](https://openai.com/policies/terms-of-use/) assign its interests in output, if any, subject to applicable law and exclude third-party output from that assignment. Those terms are not proof that copyright exists in every output or that third-party inputs were authorized. The exact account/region/employer/business terms used when generating each file were not supplied; an applicable agreement review remains an owner-side gate. No novel assignment from OpenAI is needed or implied by this draft.

The US Copyright Office [January 2025 report announcement](https://www.copyright.gov/newsnet/2025/1060.html) distinguishes human expressive contributions from purely generated material and prompting alone. That is US guidance, not a universal legal ruling. Git commit authorship, test coverage and the fact a file was AI-translated cannot certify a human copyright or a clean chain of title. The proposed scope grants only eligible rights actually held and makes no exclusive claim over unprotectable AI-only material.

## Immediate answer: which files are safe to MIT?

**No new license has been applied and no exhaustive legal clearance is certified.** Strong candidates are the newly written server/launcher/release helpers, modern repair/persistence adapters and their own tests. Exact files/hashes are individually marked as modern-infrastructure or modern-browser in file-inventory.json (71 + 17 files). These are *candidates*, not a claim that every embedded string/data element is independently owned. Nineteen port/render files and seven generated/preset runtime files are specifically withheld from a blanket grant; five screenshots and two upstream-notice files are separate. An active MIT license should use an approved file/portion scope after the mixed-file review, not simply `web/src/**`.

Established third-party permission already exists for Vite's code and the later MAE files carrying Trevor McCauley's MIT header, under their own notices; the owner is not their original copyright holder. The strongest route for direct ports is component-level permission or a documented rebase to that verified licensed source, rather than pretending the adaptation was authored without reference.
